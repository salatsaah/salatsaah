Privacy policy

Salat Sa'ah is an Islamic smart wall clock for Android, developed by the Salat Sa'ah Dev Team. This policy explains the app's data flows and your choices.

1. At a glance

The app uses your settings and location to calculate prayer times and request weather. Google Calendar is optional: it connects only with your consent and is used to show upcoming events.

The reviewed app has no developer-operated backend, advertising SDK or analytics integration. This does not mean that no data is stored or shared: preferences are stored on your device, and network services receive information needed to respond to requests.

2. Google Calendar and consent

When you choose to connect Google Calendar, Google asks you to sign in and authorize read-only access to your calendar list and calendar events. The permissions requested are calendar.calendarlist.readonly and calendar.events.readonly. You can use the other display features without connecting Calendar.

The app reads calendar names, identifiers and time zones so you can choose calendars. It requests upcoming event responses from selected calendars for the next 30 days and displays up to three upcoming events. It uses event titles, identifiers, calendar names, start and end times, all-day status and time zones. Google's responses can include additional event fields; the app does not request a restricted field subset. Calendar permission is broader than the few events visible on screen.

This access is used to display your upcoming plans, not to create, edit or delete events. Calendar events are not sent to Open-Meteo or a developer server by the reviewed code. Be aware that event titles may be visible to anyone who can see your wall-clock display.

Google's sign-in service handles authentication. The app receives access tokens to authorize direct HTTPS requests to Google; it does not ask for or store your Google password. The native Google Sign-In SDK manages the sign-in session and token lifecycle. The app stores your connected email address in Expo SecureStore and calendar selections in local preferences. It may restore a previously authorized session when reopened.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Salat Sa'ah's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. These restrict use to permitted user-facing purposes and limit transfers and human access. Google data must not be sold or used for advertising or credit decisions.

Google user data is used only to display your Calendar events. We do not sell it, use it for advertising or AI-model training, or allow staff to access it. The connected email address is used only to identify the connected account in the app, not for marketing or a mailing list. This statement does not mean Google has verified the app.

3. Location and weather

You can select a city or provide coordinates manually, or allow the app to request your device location while the app is in use. The app requests foreground location permission; the reviewed code does not request background location access. If location is unavailable, the app may use its configured fallback location, Mecca.

Prayer times are calculated on your device using coordinates and calculation settings. Weather requests send the active latitude and longitude, temperature unit and forecast options to Open-Meteo. Coordinates are sent as available, without additional rounding, even though the device request uses a low-accuracy setting. Manual city searches send the search text and language to Open-Meteo's geocoding service. Manual-location time-zone resolution can also use that service.

These services receive your IP address and other normal network-request information. Open-Meteo states that API troubleshooting logs can contain coordinates and are deleted after 90 days. See Open-Meteo's terms and privacy notice for its current practices.

4. Storage, retention and security

  • Preferences: settings, chosen calendar identifiers and saved location details persist in local app storage until replaced or removed. They are not encrypted by the app's preference-storage layer.
  • Connected account: the email address is stored using Expo SecureStore. Authentication state is also managed by Google's native SDK; the app does not set a fixed SDK-token retention period.
  • Events and weather: the reviewed code keeps responses in app memory, not in an intentionally persistent event or weather-history database. Previously fetched information can remain visible when a refresh fails. Disconnecting clears the app's Calendar state.
  • Backups: Android backup is enabled. Ordinary preferences may be included in system backups or device transfers, depending on your device settings. The configured SecureStore rules exclude its storage from those backups. Clearing an installation does not necessarily remove an existing system backup.

The app uses HTTPS for its Google and Open-Meteo requests. Device access controls and platform storage protections also matter; no system can guarantee absolute security. Google, Open-Meteo and other providers apply their own retention policies to information held by them.

5. Disconnecting and deleting data

  • Disconnect Calendar: use the Calendar disconnect control in Settings. The app clears displayed events, calendar lists and the connected account state, attempts to delete the locally saved email, and asks Google to revoke access and sign out. Saved calendar selections can remain in preferences.
  • Revoke at Google: if the device is offline or revocation fails, remove Salat Sa'ah's access through your Google Account connections. Disconnecting does not delete events stored in Google Calendar.
  • Change location: choose a manual location or revoke the app's location permission in Android Settings. Revoking permission does not automatically erase saved coordinates; changing the location replaces the saved value.
  • Remove local app data: Android Settings → Apps → Salat Sa'ah → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. This removes the installation's local settings; manage any cloud backups separately. Your Google Calendar and third-party service logs are not deleted by clearing this app.
  • Contact the team: email salatsaah.support@gmail.com for privacy questions or deletion requests relating to correspondence you sent us. For data held by Google or Open-Meteo, use that provider's privacy controls or contact channels.

6. External services and donations

Google receives authentication requests and Calendar API calls. Open-Meteo receives location-based weather and city-search requests. Their processing is subject to their respective policies, including Google's Privacy Policy.

Donation buttons open external charity websites in your browser. Salat Sa'ah does not process donations or collect payment-card information through the app. Information you enter on those websites, and browser request information, are handled by the charity and its payment providers under their own policies. Opening a link does not intentionally attach Calendar events or app coordinates.

7. This website and support email

This static website contains no analytics, tracking scripts, cookies, forms or browser-storage code. Fonts and images are served locally with the site. GitHub Pages hosts the files and may process IP addresses and request information to deliver and protect the service; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If you email support, your email provider and the team's Gmail service process your address, message and any attachments. Do not send passwords, sign-in tokens or private Calendar content. Our support policy is to use correspondence only to respond to your request and to delete the messages and attachments from the support mailbox within 90 days after the request is resolved. You may request earlier deletion by emailing support. Where retention is required by law, only the necessary information will be kept for the required period. Email providers may retain separate copies or backups under their own policies. Support correspondence is separate from the Google account email stored locally by the app.
